About Askari Horizon

SHAPING THE NEXT GENERATION OF LEADERS

Founded by experienced instructors, military veterans, and psychological evaluation experts, Askari Horizon bridges the gap between raw candidate potential and military excellence.

Our Mission & Philosophy

We Don't Just Prepare You For An Exam; We Cultivate Officer-Like Qualities (OLQs).

At Askari Horizon, we empower teenagers and young candidates aspiring to join the Armed Forces. Passing the military selection process requires more than book knowledge—it demands psychological resilience, quick decision-making under stress, clear articulation, and genuine leadership.

Our comprehensive methodology equips candidates for all three dimensions of the Inter Services Selection Board (ISSB): Psychological Assessment, Group Testing Officer (GTO) Tasks, and Deputy President Interview.

CORE ACADEMY OBJECTIVES

1
360° ISSB Preparation: Complete psychological screening, indoor/outdoor GTO tasks, and Deputy President interview mastery.
2
Initial Computerized Screening: Verbal, Non-Verbal intelligence, and Academic test drills for AS&RC / Selection Centers.
3
Standardized Outdoor GTO Ground: Live practice on authentic obstacle courses (Paddles, Planks, Ropes, Drums, Command Tasks).
4
Personalized Dossier Evaluation: One-on-one psychological counseling on WAT, TAT, SRT, and PIQ form alignment.
Structured Curriculum

ACADEMY TRAINING MODULES

Choose between our signature 4-Week Regular Batch or the intensive 6-Day Fast-Track Crash Course.

Week 1

Module 1: Initial Test & Baseline Assessment

  • • Verbal & Non-Verbal Intelligence test shortcuts.
  • • Academic GK, Pak Studies, & Islamic Studies quizzes.
  • • Computerized test practice mimicking AS&RC screens.
  • • Baseline physical fitness test (1.6km, push-ups, chin-ups).
Week 2

Module 2: Psychological Battery Masterclass

  • • Word Association Test (WAT): 75-word timed drills.
  • • Picture Story Writing (TAT) & Incomplete Sentences.
  • • Situation Reaction Tests (SRT) problem solving.
  • • Personal Information Questionnaire (PIQ) alignment.
Week 3

Module 3: GTO Tasks & Group Dynamics

  • • Group Discussions (GD) & Group Planning Exercise (GPE).
  • • Progressive Group Task (PGT) & Half Group Task (HGT).
  • • Individual Obstacles & Command Tasks execution.
  • • Lecturette 2-minute public speaking drills.
Week 4

Module 4: Interview & Full Mock ISSB

  • • Deputy President (DP) mock interview etiquette & posture.
  • • Tackling rapid-fire & stress questions.
  • • 3-Day Full Mock ISSB Simulation.
  • • Final 1-on-1 counseling & weak-point analysis.
